## Rules

- Duplicates: same account, normalized merchant, currency, exact amount and calendar date; abstain after three prior dates with at least the same number of matching purchases per day. Linked recurring, bill, vendor-payment, invoice and subscription records are excluded.
- Unusual spending: at least eight prior expenses across four prior dates, same account/category/currency, using up to 50 samples within 90 days. Amount must exceed both 3 × median and median + 6 × median absolute deviation. Same-day/future entries, user-marked one-offs and linked payments do not train the baseline.
- At most 5,000 records, 100 findings and 50 cited records per finding. Newest findings come first.

## Limits and unresolved cases

- Synthetic cases use declared review labels; these metrics are not production accuracy or fraud detection rates.
- A matching candidate requires all labeled target IDs. Other findings are reported separately and are not assigned unlabeled ground truth.
- Same-day legitimate repeats and unmarked planned purchases remain ambiguous. Habit suppression also misses some genuine duplicates.
- This V2 detector does not enqueue alerts, reverse payments, block accounts or delete transactions; existing notification rules are unchanged.

| Case | Result | Context |

## Runtime

5000 records, 5 local runs: median 91.88 ms, maximum 129.73 ms. Informational local CPU timing; no platform-independent speed guarantee.

## Release decision

**Review cards only. Automatic alerts from this V2 detector remain disabled; existing notification-rule settings are unchanged.** A broader labeled real-data sample and purchase/reference evidence would be needed before treating these synthetic results as production validation.
